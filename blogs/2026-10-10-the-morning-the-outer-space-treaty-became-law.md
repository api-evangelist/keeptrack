At 11:25 a.m. on October 10, 1967, Lyndon Johnson stood in the East Room as the Outer Space Treaty entered into force. Fifty-nine years later, its 1967 sentences decide who answers for Starlink, why ASAT debris is a legal problem, and what nobody is allowed to park in orbit.
